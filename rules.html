PICKLEBALL CLUB

House Rules

For every player in the 1st Official Mini Tournament. Please read them before your first game.

  1. 1

    Official Pairing & Game Schedule

    All players must follow their assigned partners, match schedules, and court assignments. Please be ready when your names are called to avoid delays.

  2. 2

    Game Rules & Scoring

    All matches shall follow the tournament mechanics and scoring system established by our Games & Tournament Committee. The assigned umpires will guide and officiate each match.

  3. 3

    Respect the Umpires

    Please respect the umpires’ decisions. Any concerns or clarifications should be raised respectfully through the proper tournament officials. Let’s avoid unnecessary arguments.

  4. 4

    Fair Play & Sportsmanship

    No intentional distractions, offensive language, or unsportsmanlike behavior. Whether winning or losing, always treat your partners, opponents, and officials with respect.

  5. 5

    One Player, One Paddle

    In accordance with our club policy, we strictly observe one (1) paddle per player.

  6. 6

    Safety & Hydration

    Stay hydrated, be mindful of your surroundings, and report any injuries or discomfort immediately. Water is available for everyone through our official water sponsor, Nature’s Spring.

  7. 7

    Enjoy the Game!

    Remember, this is our very first official club tournament. Winning is exciting, but friendship, teamwork, and sportsmanship are what make After 5 special.

Compete with passion. Play with respect. Celebrate as one club.

Good luck to all our players, and may the best teams win!

ONE CLUB. ONE PASSION. ONE AFTER 5!
